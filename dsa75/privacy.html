Summary: The App collects no personal data. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. All learning data is stored exclusively on your device. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App collects no personal information. For the avoidance of doubt, the App does not collect, store, transmit, or otherwise process any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number, or any other contact information. The App has no account system and never requires you to sign in.
  • Usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash reports. The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting software development kit.
  • Location data, advertising identifiers, or any data used to track you across applications or websites.
  • Any code you write in the practice IDE. Such code is executed locally on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

The foregoing is consistent with the App's privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy): tracking is disabled, and the list of collected data types is empty.

2. Information Stored Exclusively on Your Device

All data generated through your use of the App is stored solely on your device and never leaves it, including:

  • Your learning progress, daily check-ins, experience points (XP), and wrong-question book.
  • Your language preference (Simplified Chinese or English).
  • Your daily reminder preference, which is off by default. If you enable it, the App displays a local notification at 20:00; no data is transmitted in connection therewith.

Such data is stored in on-device storage and is deleted when you delete the App. The Developer operates no server, provides no synchronization service, and maintains no backup of such data.

3. Offline Operation

The App is designed to operate fully offline after installation. The Python runtime (Pyodide) is bundled within the App; accordingly, the practice IDE executes your code on your device without any network connection.
