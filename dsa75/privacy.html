Privacy Policy — DSA 75

Developer: Tao Yang (Toney) · Effective Date: November 9, 2026 · Last Updated: November 19, 2026 · App Version: 1.0.4+

This Privacy Policy (this “Policy”) describes the data practices of DSA 75 (the “App”), developed by Tao Yang, operating as Toney (the “Developer”). By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge the practices described herein. This Policy forms part of the App's Terms of Use, which are published at https://toney.pro/dsa75/terms.html and linked from Settings in the App.

Summary: The App collects no personal data. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your learning progress is stored in your private iCloud and syncs across your devices; the Developer operates no server and cannot read it. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App collects no personal information. For the avoidance of doubt, the App does not collect, store, transmit, or otherwise process any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number, or any other contact information. The App has no account system and never requires you to sign in.
  • Usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash reports. The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting software development kit.
  • Location data, advertising identifiers, or any data used to track you across applications or websites.
  • Any code you write in the practice IDE is executed locally on your device. Your code is saved as part of your learning progress and syncs to your private iCloud (see Section 2); it is never sent to the Developer or to any third-party server.

The foregoing is consistent with the App's privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy): tracking is disabled, and the list of collected data types is empty.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your learning progress, daily check-ins, experience points (XP), wrong-question book, and the code you write are stored in your private iCloud database (CloudKit) and sync automatically across your devices signed in with the same Apple Account. This data is stored on Apple's servers in your iCloud account and counts against your own iCloud storage quota. The Developer operates no server, cannot read this data, and maintains no backup of it. It is not deleted when you delete the App; it returns when you reinstall and sign in with the same Apple Account. The start time of your 3-day free period is likewise stored in your private iCloud (key-value storage), so the free period is measured from your first use on any device signed in with the same Apple Account.

Your language preference (Simplified Chinese or English) and your daily reminder preference are stored only on your device and are not synced. The reminder preference is off by default; if you enable it, the App displays a local notification at 20:00, and no data is transmitted in connection therewith.

3. Offline Operation

The App is designed to operate fully offline after installation. The Python runtime (Pyodide) is bundled within the App; accordingly, the practice IDE executes your code on your device without any network connection.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Full access to the App is sold as an auto-renewable subscription ($9.99 per month in the United States, with a 3-day (72-hour) free period automatically provided on download), purchased through the App Store using StoreKit. Payment is processed entirely by Apple: your payment details are provided to Apple, and the Developer does not receive them. You may manage or cancel your subscription at any time in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, and you may restore a purchase from within the App. Full subscription terms are available in the Subscription information in the App and in Section 2 of the Terms of Use.

5. Third-Party Links and Services

The App contains links that open in your web browser (Safari), including links to the Developer's website at https://toney.pro (this Policy, the Terms of Use, and the contact page) and to Apple's subscription management page. Such third-party websites are governed by their own privacy policies. This Policy applies solely to the App itself.

6. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal information from any user, it collects no personal information from children, including children under the age of thirteen (13).

The Developer may update this Policy from time to time. If the App begins to collect any data in a future version (for example, if analytics or crash reporting is introduced), this Policy and the App's privacy manifest shall be updated prior to the release of such version. The current version of this Policy is the version published at this address.
