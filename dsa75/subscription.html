Subscription — DSA 75 Full Access

Developer: Tao Yang (Toney8) · Effective Date: November 9, 2026 · Last Updated: November 11, 2026 · App Version: 1.0.4+ · Product: DSA 75 Full Access

This page describes the DSA 75 Full Access subscription (the “Subscription”), offered through the App Store. The terms set forth herein are consistent with, and form part of, the App's Terms of Use. In the event of any inconsistency between this page and the in-app subscription information, the App Store listing and the Terms of Use shall govern.

Summary: DSA 75 Full Access is an auto-renewable subscription at $9.99 per month (United States baseline; the price displayed in the App is the App Store price for your region). Eligible new subscribers shall receive a 3-day free trial with full access. Unless you cancel, the Subscription shall commence automatically upon expiry of the trial and shall renew monthly thereafter. You may cancel at any time in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. Payment is processed entirely by Apple.

1. What the Subscription Includes

A single Subscription unlocks the full App on iPhone, iPad, and Mac (Universal Purchase — one application, one Subscription), including:

  • All seventy-five (75) DSA problems, each presented through guided micro-questions that build toward the optimal solution.
  • The offline Python IDE, in which you may write and test your own code. The Python runtime is bundled within the App; no network connection is required after installation.
  • Your learning progress, daily check-ins, experience points (XP), and wrong-question book.

2. Price

The price displayed in the App is the App Store price for your region, presented using StoreKit's localized pricing; the United States baseline is $9.99 per month. Any applicable taxes shall be determined in accordance with the App Store rules for your region. The Developer sets no price outside the App Store.

3. Free Trial — 3 Days

  • Eligible new subscribers shall receive a 3-day free trial with full access to all seventy-five (75) problems.
  • Trial eligibility is determined by Apple, and the trial is available once per subscriber. If you have previously used a trial for this Subscription, no trial wording shall appear in the App, and the purchase button shall read “Subscribe — $9.99/month” rather than offering a trial.
  • No charge shall be incurred during the trial period.

4. Automatic Renewal After the Trial

Unless you cancel, the Subscription shall commence automatically upon expiry of the trial, and payment shall be charged to your Apple Account upon confirmation of purchase. The Subscription shall thereafter renew automatically each month, unless auto-renewal is disabled at least twenty-four (24) hours before the end of the then-current period.

5. Managing or Cancelling Your Subscription

You may manage or cancel your Subscription at any time; there is no requirement to contact the Developer:

  • On iPhone or iPad: iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > DSA 75, then disable auto-renewal or cancel.
  • On the web: https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions (signed in with the same Apple Account).
  • Within the App: “Me” > Manage Subscription.

Cancellation shall prevent future renewals only — your access shall continue until the end of the then-current paid or trial period. No further charge shall be incurred after cancellation.

6. Access Without a Subscription

If the trial period expires without a Subscription, or upon expiry of a paid period, the learning content shall be locked and the App shall display the subscription page, which may be dismissed; the “Me” tab shall remain accessible. Your locally stored progress shall be retained on your device and shall remain available if you subscribe at a later date.

7. Restoring Purchases

If you have previously subscribed, you may tap “Restore Purchases” within the App (on the subscription page or in “Me”). The App shall thereupon verify your subscription with the App Store and restore access on any device signed in with the same Apple Account. Restoring a purchase requires a network connection.

8. Refunds

Payment and refunds are handled exclusively by Apple in accordance with the App Store's policies. The Developer is unable to issue refunds directly. Refund requests should be directed to Apple Support or submitted at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
